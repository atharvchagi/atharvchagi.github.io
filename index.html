Bio

Hi! Good to meet you, and welcome to my website! I am Atharv Chagi, a BS/MS student at Texas A&M University learning Computer Science + Math.

This summer, I'll be a software engineering intern at Texas Instruments, exploring AI agents for chip design. Previously, I was a software engineering intern at Siemens working on LLM applications for Electrical Products R&D teams.

I'm currently working in the DIVE Lab advised by Dr. Shuiwang Ji (also collaborating with Dr. Dileep Kalathil and James Caverlee), developing post-training methods for Large Language Models and Diffusion Language Models.

Feel free to reach out to me via email!
